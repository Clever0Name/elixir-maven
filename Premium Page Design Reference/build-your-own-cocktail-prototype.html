Every build here follows the Sour template — roughly 50ml spirit : 20ml citrus/juice : 20ml syrup, topped after. Options that would break that balance are greyed out rather than offered. When a rich spirit (Whiskey, Cognac) meets juice or honey, a Classic / Adjusted toggle appears — full 50ml stays IBA-standard against pure citrus, Adjusted trims to 40ml and swaps the glass and garnish for a lighter build.
